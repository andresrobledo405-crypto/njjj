# Repurpose AI - Promotional Video

## Workflow
`/product-launch-video`

## Flow
`standalone` (30-second video, no external website)

---

## The Product

**Repurpose AI** - A SaaS platform that converts one long-form video into four platform-optimized clips (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn).

**Problem:** Content creators waste time manually editing and optimizing videos for each platform.

**Solution:** AI-powered one-click conversion with viral score prediction and automatic platform optimization.

---

## The Video

**Duration:** 30 seconds  
**Format:** Promotional explainer with animated UI mockups  
**Language:** English (primary) + Spanish voiceover option  
**Target:** Content creators, marketing teams, social media managers

---

## Visual Narrative

### Scene 1 (0-8s) - Problem Setup
- **Title:** "One Video. Four Platforms."
- **Visual:** User desktop with long-form video on screen
- **Animation:** Fade in, text appears with typing effect
- **Audio:** Soft upbeat electronic music

### Scene 2 (8-16s) - The Magic (AI Analysis)
- **Visual:** Video uploads to Repurpose AI interface
- **Animation:** 
  - Upload progress bar → completion
  - AI analyzer scanning animation (moving lines, glow effects)
  - Viral score calculations animating (numbers counting up)
  - Platform badges (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn) appearing with dynamic motion
- **Audio:** Music builds, subtle digital "ding" sounds on each badge

### Scene 3 (16-25s) - The Result (Clips Generated)
- **Visual:** Four clips appear in split screen
  - TikTok: Short, punchy vertical clip
  - Reels: Polished, centered, bright
  - Shorts: Educational, clean UI
  - LinkedIn: Professional, minimal
- **Animation:** 
  - Each clip appears with slide-in animation from sides
  - Staggered entrance (cascade effect)
  - Scale and fade-in with slight bounce
- **Audio:** Music reaches peak, satisfying "whoosh" transitions

### Scene 4 (25-30s) - Call-to-Action
- **Visual:** Logo + "Get Started Free" button
- **Animation:** 
  - Repurpose AI logo animates in (scale + fade)
  - Button pulses with attention-grabbing gradient animation
  - Final text: "No credit card required"
- **Audio:** Music concludes with triumphant note, smooth fade-out

## Design Specs

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Purple (#667eea) to Pink (#764ba2) gradient
- **Secondary:** Bright cyan (#00d4ff) for accents
- **Neutral:** White (#ffffff), Dark (#1a1a1a)
- **Accent:** Gold (#FFD700) for success states

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Sans-serif, bold (Segoe UI, Inter, or system)
- **Body:** Clean, readable, 16-18px minimum

### Motion Style
- **Easing:** ease-out for appearances, cubic-bezier for sophistication
- **Timing:** Snappy (200-400ms per element)
- **Bounce:** Subtle 10-15% overshoot on clip arrivals
- **Parallax:** Subtle background shifts as clips move

### UI Elements
- Modern gradient buttons
- Smooth rounded corners (6-8px border-radius)
- Glassmorphic overlays (semi-transparent backgrounds)
- Animated gradient borders
- Clean icons for platforms
